Permissions & Role Hierarchy

Understand the permissions Uranium uses and configure the bot with least privilege.

Documentation • Updated 7 November 2026

Use least privilege

Grant only permissions needed for the features you enable. A moderation bot may need different permissions depending on whether you use timeouts, message cleanup, role panels, AutoMod or voice features. Review the Discord authorization screen rather than blindly accepting a permission bundle.

Common permission mapping

  • View Channels / Read Message History: read supported command context and channel content.
  • Send Messages / Embed Links: respond with help, reports and moderation feedback.
  • Manage Messages: moderation cleanup and message-management features.
  • Moderate Members: timeouts where supported.
  • Kick Members / Ban Members: only if your staff actually use those actions.
  • Manage Roles: role assignment features, limited by role hierarchy.
  • Manage Channels: only for channel-management commands.

Role hierarchy

Uranium cannot manage a member or role that is equal to or higher than the bot’s highest role. The person invoking a command must also have the required permission. Never use the bot to bypass Discord’s permission model.
