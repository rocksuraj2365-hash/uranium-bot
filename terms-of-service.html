1. What Uranium is

Uranium is a Discord bot (built with discord.js) with an Express web dashboard. It provides server, member, message, channel, role, voice, AutoMod, information, music and optional AI-assisted commands. Features can change, and some features are restricted to the bot operator (labelled “Premium” / owner-only in the bot).

2. Acceptable use

  • Use Uranium only in servers where you have the right to be, and only with the Discord permissions your role actually gives you. Uranium’s commands check the permissions of the person running them.
  • Use moderation tools only for genuine server-safety and community-management purposes, in line with the rules of that server.
  • Keep reasons for moderation actions accurate. Reasons, warnings and notes may be saved in that server’s moderation records.
  • Only provide links, images, audio streams and other content that you have the right to use and that other members are allowed to see.

3. Prohibited activities

  • Harassing, threatening, doxxing or targeting people, or using Uranium to organise or automate abuse, raids or spam.
  • Mass bans, mass kicks, purges, lockdowns, broadcasts or other high-impact actions without a lawful, genuine moderation reason and the required permission.
  • Trying to bypass permission checks, role hierarchy, blacklists, rate limits or confirmation prompts, or trying to make another user’s action run with more permission than that user has.
  • Asking Uranium, including its AI features, to reveal passwords, Discord tokens, API keys or other secrets. Uranium never needs your Discord password or user token — never enter them into Uranium.
  • Using Uranium to break the law, Discord’s Terms of Service or Community Guidelines, or another person’s rights.
  • Attacking, probing or disrupting the bot, dashboard, hosting or any connected third-party service, except for authorised security testing agreed with the operator.

4. Server administrator responsibilities

  • Administrators decide whether Uranium belongs in their server and which roles, channels and permissions it receives. Give Uranium only the permissions it needs, and keep its role position in mind: it cannot manage roles or members at or above its highest role.
  • Administrators and moderators are responsible for the actions they start with Uranium, including bans, timeouts, purges, role changes, AutoMod rules and announcements, and for reviewing those actions afterwards.
  • Administrators should configure server settings deliberately — for example the moderation log channel, appeal link, custom prefix, AI on/off state and AI chat channel — and tell their members what Uranium records in that server.
  • Administrators must make sure their own server rules and any appeal process are clear. Uranium can show an appeal link that an administrator has set, but it does not run the appeal process for a server.

5. Moderation and automated moderation limitations

  • Uranium acts through the Discord API. An action can fail or be refused if Discord, the permissions, the role hierarchy, the channel type or Discord’s own limits do not allow it. Error messages in Discord explain the specific problem where the code can identify it.
  • AutoMod features create and manage Discord AutoMod rules. Automated rules can block legitimate messages (false positives) and can miss harmful content (false negatives). They are a helper, not a replacement for human review.
  • Some actions are scheduled, for example temporary bans, temporary roles, timed lockdowns and giveaways. Scheduling depends on the bot running and on Discord being reachable; timing is not guaranteed.
  • Bulk deletion and other Discord operations are subject to Discord’s limits. Uranium does not promise that every message, member or rule can be changed in every situation.

6. AI feature limitations

  • AI features are optional. They only work when the bot operator has configured an Anthropic API key and the server has not turned AI off.
  • AI output can be wrong, incomplete or out of date. Do not rely on it alone for moderation, safety, legal or other important decisions; a person must review important actions.
  • When AI runs a bot command, it runs as the person who asked and cannot use more permission than that person. Dangerous actions still use Uranium’s normal checks and, where the command requires it, its confirmation flow.
  • Do not put passwords, tokens, private keys or sensitive personal information into AI requests.

Uranium depends on services it does not control, including Discord, its hosting provider, Anthropic (only for AI features) and the audio-stream providers used by music features, as well as streams or links supplied by users. Those services have their own terms and availability, and Uranium is not responsible for their downtime, changes or content.
