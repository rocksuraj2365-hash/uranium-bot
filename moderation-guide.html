Moderation Guide

Practical guidance for responsible warnings, timeouts, kicks, bans and audit records.

Documentation • Updated 7 November 2026

Before taking action

  1. Check the server rule and the relevant context.
  2. Use the least severe action that addresses the issue.
  3. Write a factual, concise reason. Avoid sensitive personal details.
  4. Check target identity and role hierarchy before confirming.
  5. Record the action in a private moderation log.

Warnings and notes

Use warnings for documented rule violations and moderator notes for relevant staff context. Notes are not public announcements. Keep them factual, limited and relevant, and remove records when no longer needed under your server policy.

Timeouts, kicks and bans

Timeouts temporarily restrict a member’s ability to participate. Kicks remove a member but do not prevent rejoining. Bans prevent joining until lifted. Confirm the correct member and reason before using high-impact actions.

Appeals and human review

Make your server’s appeal route visible. Uranium can display an administrator-configured appeal link, but server staff remain responsible for reviewing appeals fairly and consistently.
