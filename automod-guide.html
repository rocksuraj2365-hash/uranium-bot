AutoMod Guide

Set up automated moderation carefully and understand false positives and limitations.

Documentation • Updated 7 November 2026

What AutoMod does

Discord AutoMod can detect configured keyword patterns and spam-like behavior and can block or alert on matching content. Uranium’s AutoMod commands configure Discord-side rules where supported; the rules are enforced by Discord.

Recommended rollout

  1. Start with a small set of clear, high-confidence rules.
  2. Send alerts to a private moderator channel.
  3. Test normal community phrases to identify false positives.
  4. Tell moderators how to review and adjust rules.
  5. Expand gradually and audit results after changes.

Limitations

Automated filters can miss harmful content and can flag harmless messages. Do not treat a match as proof of intent. Keep a human review process for serious actions and provide a way for members to appeal moderation decisions.
